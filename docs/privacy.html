Privacy

Budget is a personal budgeting app for iOS. It is used only by its owner, for their own bank accounts. It is not offered to other people.

What data is used

When you connect a bank, the app reads account details (account name, IBAN, currency) and transactions (date, amount, counterparty and description) through Enable Banking, after you approve this at your bank.

Where data is stored

All data is stored only on the iPhone where the app is installed. There is no server, no account and no analytics. Data is sent directly between the iPhone and Enable Banking and is not shared with anyone else.

How long data is kept

Data stays on the device until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Bank access ends automatically when the consent expires, or earlier if you remove the connection in the app or revoke it at your bank.

Contact

Questions about this app can be sent to the email address registered with Enable Banking for this application.
